# Design direction

Follow `docs/DESIGN_PROCESS.md` steps 1 to 4 for the app, one step at a time, and stop for James after each.

- Read the app's `SPEC.md`, `docs/BRAND.md`, `docs/WEB_STANDARDS.md` and `docs/AI_SMELL.md` first.
- References: open each site with Playwright MCP at 390px and 1440px wide, and scroll through it. Write what is worth taking, not a description of the whole site. Never copy a site; take ideas.
- The big idea comes from the business: its work, its brand, its story. Offer two or three, each in a few lines, and say which you'd pick and why.
- Write `docs/design/DIRECTION.md` only once James has chosen the idea. Keep it to a page or two.
- Prototypes are throwaway and live outside the app's code. Record each at both widths so James can compare them.
- Anything that costs money, such as a paid tool, a font licence or generated video, is listed with its cost for approval.
- Your instinct is the average site. When a choice feels safe, look again at the references and the big idea, and offer the bolder option alongside it.
